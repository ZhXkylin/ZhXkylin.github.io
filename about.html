Academic

Xi Zhang is an MA candidate in Pacific and Asian Studies at the University of Victoria. Graduate research focuses on Chinese literature, religion, gender, and contemporary media.

Work beyond the thesis includes research administration, teaching assistance, student mentoring, and public-facing service. Across these settings, the work calls for careful organization, clear writing, and communication with people from varied backgrounds.
