03 — RESEARCH

Female heroism
across text & media.

The MA thesis examines Deng Chanyu, a female warrior in the Ming novel Creation of Gods, across the original text, contemporary screen adaptations, and Chinese social media discussions.

The project considers how gender, martial authority, religion, and audience reception shape her changing representations.
